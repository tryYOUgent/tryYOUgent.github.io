Custom Church Solutions

Built Around Your Church.
Not a Pricing Tier.

Every church has different needs. ChurchCompass is customized to serve your ministry, so you only invest in the tools and services that are useful to your church.

Flexible, à la carte pricing with a custom quote after conversation. Some offerings are optional and subject to integration and channel availability, as well as setup.

What ChurchCompass Can Build

Practical ministry tools, shaped around your church.

ChurchCompass works with your team to choose the mix of public-facing experiences and behind-the-scenes systems that best fit your goals, existing tools, and budget. Some capabilities depend on integration, platform, or channel availability.

🌐
Public-Facing Digital Presence
24/7 website digital representative and visitor FAQ, church website design and landing pages, custom inquiry forms, prayer requests, care requests, visitor forms, event registration, reminders, and multilingual experiences when supported.
📞
Voice, SMS, and Follow-Up
Phone and voice answering, call routing, SMS and email follow-ups, outreach campaigns, event reminders, and social media publishing or automated responses where supported.
🧭
Staff and Ministry Operations
Ministry and volunteer internal assistants, searchable staff resources, lead and visitor connection tracking, staff notifications, workflow automations, integrations with church tools, and scheduling or appointment requests.
📚
Insights, Resources, and Tools
Dashboards, reporting, ministry insights, resource libraries, videos, handouts, and Apple or Google digital wallet passes for events and programs, depending on what your church needs and what can be supported.
Why pay for tools you won't use?

Choose only what fits your ministry.

We work with each church to identify exactly what supports your goals, your existing systems, and your budget. The result is a flexible package built from the ChurchCompass capabilities that matter most to your team.

Some churches need a polished digital front door. Others need internal workflow support, staff tools, outreach automations, or reporting. We tailor the solution so you can start with what is useful now and add more only when it makes sense.

Implementation scope is discussed individually so the solution reflects your church's actual needs and the channels and integrations that are available.

Frequently Asked

How custom solutions conversations work.

We start by learning what your church is trying to accomplish, what tools you already use, and where you need the most support. From there, we recommend a tailored path forward and note any options that depend on integrations or channel support.

🤝
What happens first?
We have a conversation about your ministry goals, current systems, and the kinds of experiences you want to create for your church and community.
🧩
Is everything included?
No. ChurchCompass is not bundled as a one-size-fits-all package. We recommend only the tools and services that are relevant to your church.
🔌
Can it connect to our existing tools?
Often, yes. We review what is possible based on your platforms, integration options, and the channels you want to support.
📝
How is pricing determined?
Pricing is flexible and à la carte. After the conversation, we prepare a custom quote based on the scope that makes sense for your church.
